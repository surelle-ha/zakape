# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Zakape welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

Examples of positive behavior include using welcoming language, respecting different viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, sexualized language, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code. Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact method listed by the repository owner. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
