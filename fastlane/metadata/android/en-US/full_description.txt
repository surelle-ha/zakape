Zakape is a local-first pixel art and sprite animation workbench built for focused drawing on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

Create artwork without an account or cloud service. Projects stay on your device and can be exported as portable Zakape project files.

Features include:

* Pencil, eraser, fill, line, rectangle, circle, mirror, and dithering tools
* Rectangular and lasso selections with move, resize, and rotation controls
* Independent layers with visibility, opacity, and rename controls
* Rearrangeable animation frames, per-frame timing, onion skinning, and live preview
* RGBA, greyscale, and indexed color modes
* Built-in palettes, custom palettes, primary and secondary drawing colors
* PNG, sprite-sheet, JSON, GIF, and project-file exports
* Touch-friendly zooming and a responsive phone and tablet workspace

The optional art assistant can connect to a model endpoint chosen by the user, including a local Ollama service. Model output is converted into reviewable pixel edits before it changes a project. The editor and every core drawing feature work without the assistant or a network connection.
